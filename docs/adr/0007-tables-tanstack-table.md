# 0007: Tables: TanStack Table v9

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-05

## What it is for

Data tables: sorting, filtering and paging logic, headless, rendered with our own components.

## Why it was chosen

Headless, so it follows `docs/design.md`, and it belongs to the family the rest of the stack uses.

## What was not chosen and why

- A component grid (AG Grid): brings its own look and far more than this product needs.
